**Checkpointing can waste up to 80+% of training time at scale.**

You can say, "What the hell Nehil! Yesterday you told me, I need to add all this stuff to the checkpoint now you tell it it wastes resources?"

Dont worry here is the solution, **async checkpointing cuts overhead from 40min to <1sec.**

Lets talk about how to checkpoint without killing throughput.

## The bottleneck is synchronous I/O

Default way blocks your training loop:

- GPU → CPU: transfer model state
- CPU → Disk: write to storage
- Training waits for both steps to finish

At scale, one checkpoint takes **30–40 minutes**.

The problem as always is: Your **$600/hour GPU cluster** sits idle.

## Async checkpointing splits this into 2 steps

**Step 1 (Sync, Fast): GPU → CPU transfer.** Blocks training briefly (~1 second). Model state moves to CPU memory.

**Step 2 (Async, Slow): CPU → Disk upload.** Background thread handles I/O. Training continues immediately.

Takes 30–40min but you don't wait. Your perceived overhead drops from 40min to <1sec.

**That's 10x–23x speedup.**

The Ray Train pattern handles this.

## Pre-reqs for this to work

- **CPU RAM buffer** (checkpoint must fit in memory)
- **Storage I/O bandwidth** (must drain before next checkpoint)
- Each `report()` blocks until previous upload completes (prevents memory overflow)

This unlocks frequent checkpointing. Checkpoint every 100 steps, every 10 steps, even every step.

No performance penalty.

**What's your checkpoint overhead? Measure it. Switch to async if it's >10 seconds.**
